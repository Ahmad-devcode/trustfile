Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Your files never leave your device

TrustFile converts and processes files entirely in your web browser, on your own device. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, never stored, and never shared with anyone. When you close the page, your files and results are gone.

What we collect

We do not require accounts, and we do not collect the files you convert. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information (such as pages visited and browser type) for security and reliability. We do not use this to identify you or to track your files.

Third-party libraries

Some tools load open-source JavaScript libraries from public content delivery networks (CDNs) so the conversion can run in your browser. Your files are not sent to these CDNs — only the library code is downloaded to your device.

Cookies

TrustFile does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store ordinary technical data (such as cached files) to make the site load faster.
