Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

This policy explains what TrustFile does - and does not do - with your files and information. In short: your files never leave your device.

Your files

When you use a TrustFile converter, your file is read and converted entirely in your web browser, on your own device. Your file is never uploaded to a server, never stored, never shared with anyone, and never used for any other purpose. When you close the page or choose another file, the previous file is gone from memory.

Accounts and personal information

TrustFile does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any personal information to use the converters. If you contact us by email, we only use your message to reply to you.

Cookies and analytics

TrustFile does not set advertising cookies and does not track your documents. Basic, privacy-friendly visit counts may be used to understand which tools are popular. No file contents or file names are ever collected.

Third-party libraries

Conversion libraries (for PDF, Word, and spreadsheet handling) are loaded from public content delivery networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr, SheetJS). These services may see standard technical information such as your IP address when your browser downloads the library files, as with any website that loads public resources.

Children's privacy

TrustFile is a general-purpose utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
