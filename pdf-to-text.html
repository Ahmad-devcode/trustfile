Extract all the text from a PDF into a clean, plain text file. Ideal for copying content into notes, feeding text into another tool, or reading a document without a PDF reader.

Frequently asked questions

Will the text keep its original layout?

The output is plain text, so columns, tables, and exact spacing are simplified. The words and their order are preserved.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

No. Scanned PDFs contain images, not selectable text. This tool extracts embedded text only; scanned pages need OCR software.

Is PDF to text free?

Yes - free and unlimited, with no signup.

What can I do with the TXT file?

Open it in Notepad, Notes, or any editor, paste it into a document, or use it with other software that reads plain text.
