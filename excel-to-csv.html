Convert Excel spreadsheets to CSV or JSON in seconds. CSV is the universal format for importing data into databases, CRMs, and other software - and JSON is ready for developers and web apps.

  1. Step 1. Upload your Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file - drag and drop it or click to browse (up to 50 MB).

Frequently asked questions

Which sheet is converted?

The first sheet of your workbook is converted. If your workbook has several sheets, save the one you need as its own file first.

Will formulas be converted?

The converted file contains the values from your spreadsheet, not the formulas themselves.

Can I convert CSV back to Excel?

Yes. Upload a CSV file and choose XLSX as the output format on this same tool.

Is my spreadsheet uploaded?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser on your device.
