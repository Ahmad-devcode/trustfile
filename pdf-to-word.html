Frequently asked questions

Will my PDF layout be preserved in Word?

Yes. With the layout option on (the default), every line of text is placed at its original position on a page of the same size, with its font size and bold styling kept — so CVs, two-column layouts and forms look the same in Word as they did in the PDF. The text stays fully editable. Photos, logos and background graphics are not carried over yet.

Can it convert a scanned PDF?

Yes. If a page has no selectable text, TrustFile automatically reads it as an image with built-in text recognition (OCR). This takes longer — especially the first time, when the recognition data downloads — and works best on clear, straight scans. Handwriting and very blurry scans may not read well.

Is PDF to Word really free?

Yes. TrustFile is completely free with no signup, no daily limit, and no watermark. The tool runs in your browser, so there is nothing to pay for.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your file is processed locally in your browser and is never uploaded, stored, or shared. When you close the page, nothing remains.

What is the maximum file size?

Files up to 50 MB are supported. Larger files may take longer depending on your device.
