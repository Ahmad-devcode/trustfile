Frequently asked questions

Is PDF to Word conversion really free?

Yes. TrustFile is completely free with no signup, no daily conversion limit, and no watermark. The conversion runs in your browser, so there is nothing to pay for.

Will my PDF layout and formatting be preserved?

The converter extracts the text from your PDF and rebuilds it as an editable Word document. Plain text and paragraph structure are preserved, but complex layouts, columns, and exact fonts may not match the original. For simple documents, reports, and letters, the result is clean and easy to edit.

Can I convert a scanned PDF?

Scanned PDFs are images of pages, not text. TrustFile cannot read text from scanned pages (that needs OCR software). If your PDF has selectable text, it will convert. If it is a scan, the output may be empty.

Is my PDF private?

Yes. Your file is processed locally in your browser and is never uploaded, stored, or shared. When you close the page, nothing remains.
