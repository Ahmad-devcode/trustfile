Frequently asked questions

Should I choose PNG or JPG?

Choose PNG for sharp text, screenshots, diagrams, and anything you will edit further. Choose JPG for photos and smaller file sizes when sharing online.

Is PDF to PNG free?

Yes. Unlimited conversions, no account, no watermark.

What resolution are the PNG images?

Pages are rendered at 2x scale, giving sharp, detailed images suitable for screens and printing.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser on your own device.
