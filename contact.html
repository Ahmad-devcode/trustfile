Before you write

  • Scanned PDFs: scanned pages are images, not text. TrustFile cannot extract text from scans - that needs OCR software.
  • PDF to Word layout: the Word output contains extracted text; exact page layout is not preserved. This is a known browser limit, stated on the tool page.
  • Large files: files up to 50 MB are supported. Very large files may be slow on older devices.
