About TrustFile

TrustFile is a free online file converter and PDF toolkit built on one simple idea: your files should never have to leave your device.

Most converter websites upload your documents to a server, process them there, and keep copies for some time. TrustFile does the opposite. Every tool — merging PDFs, converting Word to PDF, turning spreadsheets into tables, converting images — runs entirely in your web browser using modern browser technology. When you close the page, nothing remains.

What you can do here

  • PDF tools: merge, split, compress, rotate, delete pages, add page numbers, and add watermarks.
  • Convert from PDF: PDF to Word, JPG, PNG, text, and Excel.
  • Convert to PDF: Word, Excel, JPG, TXT, CSV, and HTML to PDF.
  • Images & documents: image format conversion, TXT to Word, Word to text, and Excel to CSV.

Our promise

  • Free forever: no signup, no daily limits, no watermarks.
  • Private by design: no uploads, no storage, no tracking of your files.
  • Honest limits: when a conversion cannot run properly in a browser, we say so instead of failing silently.

Questions, bug reports, or a format you would like added? Contact us — we read every message.
