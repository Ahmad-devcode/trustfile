About TrustFile

TrustFile is a free online file converter built around one simple idea: your files should never have to leave your device.

Most converter websites upload your documents to a server, process them somewhere else, and ask you to trust that they delete them afterwards. TrustFile takes a different path. Every conversion runs in your own web browser, using modern web technology. Your PDF, Word document, spreadsheet, or photo is read locally, converted locally, and downloaded locally. Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is stored. Nothing is shared.

What you can do here

TrustFile converts between the formats people use every day: PDF to Word, PDF to JPG and PNG, PDF to text, images to PDF, PNG to JPG, text to PDF and Word, Word to text, and Excel and CSV conversions. Every tool is free, with no account, no daily limit, and no watermark.

What we will not do

We will not claim to support conversions that do not work properly. Some conversions - such as PDF to Word with pixel-perfect layout, or PowerPoint to PDF - need a full office engine on a server and cannot be done well in a browser. Where a conversion has limits, we say so plainly on the tool's page, instead of giving you a broken file.
