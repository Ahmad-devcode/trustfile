Frequently asked questions

Do charts, tables and SmartArt convert?

Tables convert with their text, fills, merged cells and grid. Charts, SmartArt and animations need a full presentation renderer, so they are skipped and the result tells you how many were skipped. Text, pictures, shapes, bullets, theme colours and background colours convert with their layout intact. Banding from built-in table styles is not applied, gradient fills show as a solid colour, and shadows are not rendered.

Is PowerPoint to PDF really free?

Yes. TrustFile is completely free with no signup, no daily limit, and no watermark. The tool runs in your browser, so there is nothing to pay for.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your file is processed locally in your browser and is never uploaded, stored, or shared. When you close the page, nothing remains.

What is the maximum file size?

Files up to 50 MB are supported. Larger files may take longer depending on your device.
