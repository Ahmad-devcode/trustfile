Frequently asked questions

Which image format should I choose?

JPG for photos and sharing, PNG for screenshots and editing, WebP for websites where small file size matters most.

Does converting change image quality?

JPG and WebP use compression, so files get smaller with minimal visible change at high quality. PNG is lossless and keeps every pixel.

Is the image converter free?

Yes - free and unlimited, with no signup.

Are my images private?

Yes. Images are converted on your device and never uploaded.
