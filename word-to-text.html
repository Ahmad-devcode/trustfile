Frequently asked questions

Will images from my Word file be included?

No. This tool extracts text only. Images, charts, and embedded objects are not included in the TXT output.

Does it work with .doc files?

This tool supports modern .docx files. Older .doc files should first be saved as .docx in Word.

Is Word to text free?

Yes - free and unlimited, with no account.

Is my document uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
