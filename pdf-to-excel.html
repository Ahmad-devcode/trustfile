Frequently asked questions

Will complex tables convert perfectly?

Simple, well-spaced tables convert cleanly. Complex layouts with merged cells or tiny spacing may need a quick tidy-up in Excel afterwards — the data itself is all there.

Can it read a scanned PDF table?

Yes — scanned pages are read with built-in text recognition (OCR) first, then arranged into rows and columns. Clear scans with well-spaced tables give the best results.

Is PDF to Excel really free?

Yes. TrustFile is completely free with no signup, no daily limit, and no watermark. The tool runs in your browser, so there is nothing to pay for.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your file is processed locally in your browser and is never uploaded, stored, or shared. When you close the page, nothing remains.

What is the maximum file size?

Files up to 50 MB are supported. Larger files may take longer depending on your device.
